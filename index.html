Getting Started

I started going to the gym to build energy, get stronger, and clear my head between classes. The first week felt awkward, but a simple plan made it easy to show up.

Beginner basics

  • Wear: comfortable shirt, shorts or joggers, and flat-soled shoes.
  • Bring: a water bottle, a small towel, and headphones.
  • Warm up: five minutes of light cardio, then easy arm and leg circles.
  • Go slow: start with light weights and focus on good form.

Tips for Beginners

Start with a plan

Pick three days a week and write down your exercises before you walk in.

Form before weight

Learn the movement with light weights. Add more only when it feels easy.

Rest and refuel

Sleep, water, and protein help your muscles recover between workouts.

Ask for help

Gym staff and regulars are usually happy to show you how a machine works.

Track your progress

A simple log shows how far you've come, even when it feels slow.
